Sentinel

Memory

Knowledge

Private by architecture.

Iris's memory — every screenshot, every transcript, everything she's learned about your life — lives in a database on your Mac, nowhere else. Screens are only captured when you ask or while you've told her to watch, always with a visible flash, glow, or menu-bar pill so nothing is ever secret. One switch turns memory off. One button erases all of it. Your key, your machine, your companion.

Questions people ask.

Is she listening all the time?

While she's awake and unmuted, yes — that's what makes her a companion instead of an app you open. The menu-bar pill always tells the truth: green means listening, red means muted, a moon means asleep. Asleep means fully disconnected.

What leaves my Mac?

Your voice and, when you ask her to look, your screen images go to the AI model to be understood in the moment — the same way any assistant works. What's different is storage: Iris's memory of you lives only on your Mac, and you can wipe it in one click.

Why does macOS warn me when I install?

This early build isn't yet notarized with Apple, so the first open needs a right-click → Open. Signed builds are coming.

Windows?

The app is built cross-platform and a Windows version is on the way.

What does she cost to run?

On the free plan you bring your own Gemini key and pay Google directly — typically pennies per hour of conversation. Iris shows you a live meter so there are never surprises.
