FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Vibe Chat? +
Vibe Chat is an audio companion app for Mac that listens and speaks while your coding agent works. It tracks diffs, runs test suites, and gives you voice insights to guide your agent.
Does Vibe Chat write my code? +
No. Your AI coding agent (like Claude) writes the code. Vibe Chat is dedicated to holding the conversation around the code, observing changes, and helping you steer effectively.
Is my code kept private? +
Yes. Vibe Chat runs locally on your Mac. It reads code directly from your disk with zero cloud storage of your codebase.
