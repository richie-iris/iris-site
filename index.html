FAQ

Asked, answered.

Does Spotter write my code?

No. Your coding agent writes the code — Claude, or whatever model you run. Spotter is the voice that watches that work: it reads the diff, runs your tests, and explains what happened so you can direct the agent better. Keeping the two jobs separate is the whole point.

Which coding agents does it work with?

Any agent working in your project. Spotter watches the files, the diff, and your test harness rather than plugging into one specific tool, so it sits happily beside Claude or another model in your editor or terminal.

Can it see my screen without me asking?

No. Watching your screen is a capability you toggle on the Tools & access surface, and it's off by default. Even when it's on, Spotter only looks at the window you name, at the moment you ask. Spotter always knows which capabilities are active, and so do you.

Where does my code go?

It stays on your Mac. Spotter reads your project locally to answer your questions. There's no account and nothing is synced to a server to make it work.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple Silicon. The download is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings. It's free and needs no account to get started.
